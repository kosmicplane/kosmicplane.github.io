INTERESTS & HOBBIES

Beyond research.

Aviation, travel, nature, and physical training are important parts of how I explore, learn, and maintain discipline outside engineering.

01

Aviation & Flight Training

Flying is both a technical interest and a personal one. I enjoy general aviation, flight training, cockpit procedures and seeing aerodynamic, navigation and weather decisions play out in the real operating environment.

02

Travel & Exploration

I enjoy discovering new places, cultures and landscapes. Travel and nature are closely tied to the same curiosity that drives my engineering work: understanding unfamiliar environments, observing how systems behave, and learning from direct experience.

03

Training & Swimming

Strength training and swimming are part of my regular routine. I value the consistency, recovery, and incremental progress that physical training requires, and I find that discipline carries naturally into research and flight training.
