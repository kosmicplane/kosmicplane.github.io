ABOUT ME

Building aerospace systems that remain capable when conditions stop being ideal.

Aeronautical Engineering graduate and incoming Aerospace Engineering M.S. student focused on safety-critical and resilient autonomy, multi-robot systems, communication-aware robotics, nonlinear and optimization-based control, and simulation-to-hardware validation.

My work spans control theory, networked autonomy, embedded sensing, aerospace experimentation, digital twins and hardware validation. The central question is consistent across projects: how can an autonomous vehicle preserve safety and mission capability when the environment, sensing, communications or mission assumptions change?
